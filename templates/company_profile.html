About Unirise Barcode

Established in 2015, Unirise Techno Services Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted provider of industrial weighing, barcode identification, and automation solutions. We specialize in delivering high-quality equipment and services to industries such as manufacturing, retail, logistics, warehousing, pharmaceuticals, and FMCG.

As an authorized dealer of Essae-Teraoka and a Legal Metrology-approved company, we offer a wide range of precision weighing systems along with complete support including installation, calibration, and maintenance.

Through our division, Unirise Barcode Solutions Pvt. Ltd., we provide advanced AIDC technologies such as barcode printers, scanners, RFID systems, and labeling solutions in partnership with leading global brands.

With a strong focus on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we deliver integrated solutions that enhance operational efficiency, accuracy, and productivity for modern businesses.
